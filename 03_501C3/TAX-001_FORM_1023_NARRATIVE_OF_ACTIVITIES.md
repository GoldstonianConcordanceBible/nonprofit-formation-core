1. ORGANIZATIONAL OVERVIEW

The Tabernacle, Inc. is being organized as a Christian religious, charitable, and educational nonprofit corporation.

The organization plans to carry out four principal program activities:

1. 24/7 Christian Ministry and Community Refuge
2. Homelessness, Hunger, and Basic-Needs Relief
3. Education
4. Prison, Jail, and Reentry Outreach

The Tabernacle may acquire or lease churches and other suitable facilities from which these programs will operate.

The acquisition and operation of facilities will support the organization’s charitable programs rather than constitute an independent commercial real-estate activity.

At the time of this narrative, The Tabernacle is in its formation and program-development stage.

Programs will begin as appropriate legal, financial, insurance, staffing, facility, and institutional requirements are completed.

⸻

2. ACTIVITY ONE — CHRISTIAN MINISTRY AND COMMUNITY REFUGE

Activity

The Tabernacle will conduct Christian religious ministry through activities such as:

* Public worship
* Prayer
* Bible study
* Scripture instruction
* Religious lectures
* Discipleship
* Pastoral care
* Spiritual mentoring
* Chaplaincy
* Community prayer
* Christian fellowship
* Religious education
* Community outreach

The organization intends eventually to operate one or more physical locations that may remain accessible for extended periods or 24 hours per day where legally permissible and operationally feasible.

A 24-hour location is intended to provide a continuing place for prayer, ministry, community assistance, and peaceful refuge.

The organization will not represent a facility as an overnight shelter or residence unless that use has been lawfully approved.

⸻

Who Conducts the Activity

Programs may be conducted by:

* Ministers
* Chaplains
* Employees
* Program directors
* Qualified contractors
* Trained volunteers
* Guest speakers
* Approved community partners

Leadership will establish volunteer screening, safeguarding, and operating requirements appropriate to each role.

⸻

Where the Activity Will Occur

Activities may initially occur through:

* Leased facilities
* Partner churches
* Community locations
* Online educational platforms
* Virtual meetings

As resources become available, The Tabernacle intends to acquire churches or other appropriate buildings for its programs.

Each property will be evaluated for zoning, occupancy, fire safety, insurance, accessibility, and intended use before program operations commence.

⸻

When the Activity Will Occur

Religious programming will occur on regular schedules established by organizational leadership.

As staffing and facilities permit, certain locations may eventually operate on extended or continuous schedules.

Twenty-four-hour operation will begin only when legally authorized and appropriately staffed.

3. ACTIVITY TWO — HOMELESSNESS, HUNGER, AND BASIC-NEEDS RELIEF

Activity

The Tabernacle will provide charitable assistance to individuals and families experiencing:

* Homelessness
* Hunger
* Food insecurity
* Poverty
* Displacement
* Unemployment
* Reentry hardship
* Temporary crisis
* Other forms of distress

Initial services may include:

* Prepared meals
* Food distribution
* Bottled water and beverages
* Clothing
* Hygiene products
* Personal-care supplies
* Resource information
* Housing referrals
* Employment referrals
* Transportation-resource referrals
* Identification-document assistance
* Benefits-resource navigation
* Community-service referrals
* Mentoring
* Other basic-needs support

Where facilities, law, insurance, and licensing permit, The Tabernacle may later offer temporary refuge or other enhanced services.

No overnight shelter service will begin merely because The Tabernacle owns a church building.

⸻

Food Program

The Tabernacle plans to establish an organized charitable meal program.

Meals will be produced from standardized recipes and operating procedures.

The food program will maintain appropriate controls concerning:

* Food purchasing
* Food storage
* Food preparation
* Sanitation
* Allergens
* Portion sizes
* Temperature control
* Volunteer/staff responsibilities
* Applicable local food requirements

Recipes developed by organizational personnel may be standardized so that trained staff, temporary workers, or volunteers can consistently prepare meals.

⸻

Eligibility

The organization expects to serve people based primarily on charitable need.

Assistance will not be limited to relatives, founders, directors, donors, employees, or another predetermined private group.

Emergency or humanitarian assistance will not be administered for the purpose of generating private commercial benefit.

⸻

Religious Participation

The Tabernacle is explicitly Christian and may make religious programming available to persons receiving charitable assistance.

Where required by applicable law, government funding conditions, correctional rules, contracts, or organizational policy, participation in explicitly religious activities will be voluntary.

The organization will not structure humanitarian assistance as an improper quid pro quo for conversion or religious profession.

8. TWENTY-FOUR-HOUR OPERATIONS

The organization’s long-term operating model contemplates certain locations being available 24 hours per day.

The purpose of extended-hour operations is to provide consistent religious ministry and charitable access rather than to operate a commercial enterprise.

Twenty-four-hour operation will not commence until the applicable facility has:

* Required occupancy authority
* Appropriate insurance
* Adequate staffing
* Security procedures
* Emergency procedures
* Fire/life-safety controls
* Sanitation procedures
* Applicable food-service approvals
* Any approvals required for overnight occupancy

14. FEES

Many planned services will be offered without charge.

If The Tabernacle later charges fees for particular educational or other exempt-purpose activities, it will evaluate:

* Whether fees are reasonable
* Whether the activity remains consistent with exempt purposes
* Whether charitable access should be provided
* Whether fee revenue creates unrelated-business considerations

The organization does not presently plan to operate primarily through commercial fees.
