8. PUBLIC CHARITY CLASSIFICATION

Working Classification

The Tabernacle presently intends to seek classification as a publicly supported charity under:

Internal Revenue Code §§509(a)(1) and 170(b)(1)(A)(vi)

This working classification is based on the expectation that substantial financial support will come from:

* Members of the general public
* Individual donors
* Churches
* Community organizations
* Foundations
* Other public charities
* Government grants where appropriate

This classification shall be confirmed against actual and projected support before Form 1023 is submitted.

⸻

9. CHURCH CLASSIFICATION

The Tabernacle shall not automatically claim classification as a church under §170(b)(1)(A)(i) merely because it:

* Uses the word Christian
* Conducts worship
* Purchases a church building
* Operates from a church
* Conducts ministry

Whether the organization qualifies as a church for federal tax purposes depends upon its actual organizational and operational characteristics.

The Board may revisit church classification if The Tabernacle later develops the characteristics supporting that classification.

⸻

10. ALTERNATIVE PUBLIC-SUPPORT CLASSIFICATION

If The Tabernacle later derives substantial revenue from exempt-purpose program services, fees, or other qualifying gross receipts, the organization shall evaluate whether classification under:

Section 509(a)(2)

more accurately reflects its sources of support.

The classification chosen for the application must correspond to the organization’s good-faith financial projections.
