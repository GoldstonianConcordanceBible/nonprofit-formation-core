ARTICLE I — PURPOSE

This document establishes the accounting architecture for The Tabernacle, Inc.

The accounting system shall allow the organization to determine:

* What money was received
* Where money came from
* Whether money is donor restricted
* Which program received or used resources
* Which property incurred costs
* Which grant paid for costs
* Who authorized expenditures
* What was purchased
* Whether costs are program, management/general, or fundraising
* What assets and liabilities the organization owns or owes
* How much each charitable program costs
* Whether restricted resources have been properly used

⸻

ARTICLE IV — ACCOUNTING BASIS

The working accounting standard for organizational development shall be:

ACCRUAL-BASED ACCOUNTING

unless the Board, CPA, or accountant determines that another method is more appropriate.

Under accrual accounting:

* Revenue may be recognized according to applicable accounting rules rather than only when cash moves.
* Expenses and liabilities may be recorded when incurred rather than only when paid.
* Property and equipment are recorded as assets when appropriate.
* Depreciation may be recorded.
* Receivables and payables may be recognized.

Accounting-policy decisions shall ultimately be confirmed by the organization’s accounting professional.

6. Evaluate control failure.

Do not hide the transaction.
