ARTICLE I — PURPOSE

This policy establishes the insurance and risk-financing framework for The Tabernacle, Inc.

The organization shall align insurance with its actual activities, including:

* Governance
* Christian ministry
* Volunteers
* Employees
* Homelessness relief
* Food preparation and distribution
* Education
* Prison and reentry programming
* Fundraising
* Vehicles
* Technology
* Real estate
* Renovation
* Security
* 24/7 operations
* Potential overnight refuge

⸻

ARTICLE II — CORE RULE

HAVING INSURANCE ≠ HAVING THE RIGHT INSURANCE

No leader shall rely merely on:

“We have liability insurance.”

The organization must determine:

* What policy exists
* Who is insured
* What activities are covered
* What locations are covered
* What limits apply
* What deductibles apply
* What exclusions apply
* Whether volunteers are covered
* Whether employees are covered
* Whether abuse allegations are covered
* Whether vehicles are covered
* Whether professional services are covered
* Whether overnight operations are disclosed
* Whether armed security is disclosed
* Whether claims-made reporting deadlines apply

⸻

ARTICLE III — INSURANCE DOES NOT REPLACE RISK MANAGEMENT

Insurance transfers some financial risk.

It does not substitute for:

* Safeguarding
* Training
* Fire safety
* Food safety
* Background screening
* Financial controls
* Property maintenance
* Cybersecurity
* Worker classification
* Supervision
* Emergency response

INSURED ≠ SAFE

⸻

ARTICLE IV — INSURANCE BROKER

The Tabernacle should seek an insurance agent or broker experienced with:

* Nonprofits
* Religious organizations
* Human services
* Vulnerable populations
* Food programs
* Real estate
* Volunteers

The broker shall receive accurate descriptions of actual operations.

⸻

ARTICLE V — NO CONCEALMENT OF OPERATIONS

The Tabernacle shall not obtain a lower premium by concealing material operations such as:

* Homelessness services
* Food preparation
* Children
* Correctional work
* Counseling
* Transportation
* 24/7 access
* Overnight sleeping
* Security
* Armed security

LOWER PREMIUM ≠ COVERAGE IF MATERIAL FACTS WERE HIDDEN

OPTION A — BUY COVERAGE

OPTION B — CHANGE PROGRAM DESIGN

OPTION C — TRANSFER RISK CONTRACTUALLY WHERE LAWFUL

OPTION D — SELF-INSURE WITH EXPLICIT BOARD ACCEPTANCE

OPTION E — DO NOT LAUNCH

Do not simply ignore the exclusion.
