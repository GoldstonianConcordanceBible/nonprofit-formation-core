1. PURPOSE

This SOP establishes the exact process The Tabernacle, Inc. shall use to identify, evaluate, negotiate, acquire, and transition its first church or other ministry property.

The objective is not merely to buy a church.

The objective is to acquire a property that can lawfully, safely, financially, and sustainably support the mission.

FINDING A CHURCH ≠ BUYING A CHURCH

No individual may bind The Tabernacle to acquire real property without delegated authority.

* Property Director

11. STAGE 1 — INITIAL SCREENING

Complete a rapid screen before spending significant resources.

Answer:

Mission

Can the building realistically support The Tabernacle’s programs?

Geography

Does the location fit the intended community?

Price

Is the asking price remotely within acquisition capacity?

Condition

Does the property appear usable or reasonably repairable?

Use

Is the current or historical use potentially compatible?

Size

Is there enough space?

Parking / Access

Can people reasonably reach it?

Kitchen

Does food programming appear feasible?

Risk

Are there obvious red flags?

* Cemetery rights
* Shared access

32. ENVIRONMENTAL REVIEW

Determine whether a Phase I Environmental Site Assessment is appropriate.

For a material real-estate acquisition, especially older or previously commercial/industrial properties, professional environmental review should be strongly considered.

Where AAI/CERCLA protections are relevant, make sure the required inquiry is completed before acquisition.

81. NO-GO PROCEDURE

If a property fails:

1. Document reason.
2. Exercise contractual termination rights before deadline where applicable.
3. Recover earnest money if entitled.
4. Pay valid outstanding diligence costs.
5. Archive file.
6. Record lessons learned.
7. Return to property pipeline.

NO-GO ≠ FAILURE

A NO-GO can represent successful due diligence.

86. PROPERTY DECISION DOCTRINE

Every site must answer:

CAN WE OWN IT?

Title and financing.

MAY WE USE IT?

Zoning, occupancy, licensing.

CAN WE MAKE IT SAFE?

Building and fire/life safety.

CAN WE INSURE IT?

Risk transfer.

CAN WE AFFORD IT?

Capital and operations.

DOES IT ADVANCE THE MISSION?

Program fit.

All six matter.
