1. PURPOSE

This plan governs the first 90 days of active organizational development following legal formation.

The objectives are to:

* Stabilize governance
* Complete federal exemption filing
* Establish controlled fundraising
* Prove at least one charitable program
* Begin education and reentry pipelines
* Establish a disciplined property funnel
* Build operating reserves
* Hire only when justified
* Reach a defensible GO / NO-GO point for the first serious church offer

⸻

2. 90-DAY OPERATING PHILOSOPHY

The first 90 days shall follow:

CONTROL

PROVE

MEASURE

89. RED-LIGHT CONDITIONS

Do not make a property offer if any of these remain materially unresolved:

* No functioning Board
* No organizational bank account
* No accounting system
* Significant cash commingling
* No property authority
* No funding path
* No due-diligence budget
* Serious unresolved founder conflict
* No ability to estimate operating cost
