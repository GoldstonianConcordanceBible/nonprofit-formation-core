Nonprofit Formation Core

Status: DRAFT — NOT FILED — ATTORNEY/CPA REVIEW REQUIRED

This repository is the controlled formation, governance, compliance, and operating record for a proposed Christian religious and charitable nonprofit organization.

The organization is being established to advance religion, relieve the poor and distressed, advance education, serve incarcerated and formerly incarcerated people, and acquire and operate facilities dedicated to those purposes.

Core Mission

To establish and operate places of Christian ministry, refuge, education, service, and restoration that may remain accessible 24 hours a day where legally permitted and operationally safe.

The organization will serve people through four primary mission areas.

1. Christian Ministry

The organization may provide:

* Christian worship
* Prayer
* Bible study
* Scripture teaching
* Pastoral care
* Discipleship
* Religious education
* Community ministry
* Spiritual mentorship
* Christian outreach

Facilities may operate for extended hours or 24 hours per day where legally permitted, properly staffed, insured, and compliant with applicable building, fire, zoning, occupancy, and safety requirements.

2. Homelessness, Hunger, and Humanitarian Relief

The organization may provide assistance to people experiencing homelessness, hunger, poverty, displacement, or other forms of distress.

Programs may include:

Educational programming may include:

Organizational Character

The organization is intended to operate exclusively for religious, charitable, and educational purposes described in Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

The organization shall not be operated for the private benefit of its founders, directors, officers, employees, donors, related businesses, or other private persons.

Reasonable compensation may be paid for legitimate services actually provided to the organization when properly reviewed, approved, and documented.

⸻

Foundational Control Rules

DRAFT ≠ ADOPTED

A draft document has no governing authority merely because it appears in this repository.

ADOPTED ≠ FILED

Board approval does not substitute for a government filing when filing is required.

FILED ≠ APPROVED

Submission to a government agency does not mean the requested status has been granted.

PROPERTY OWNED ≠ PROPERTY PERMITTED

Owning a building does not establish legal authorization for shelter, overnight occupancy, food service, education, or 24/7 use.

CAN ≠ MAY

Operational capability does not itself create legal or governance authority.

Formation Sequence

The organization shall proceed in the following order:

1. Select the legal nonprofit name
2. Select the state of incorporation
3. Confirm registered agent
4. Identify initial Board of Directors
5. Draft state-compliant Articles of Incorporation
6. Draft Bylaws
7. Adopt conflict-of-interest and governance policies
8. File Articles of Incorporation
9. Obtain EIN
10. Hold organizational Board meeting
11. Elect officers
12. Adopt Bylaws
13. Adopt governance policies
14. Establish nonprofit bank account
15. Establish accounting controls
16. Prepare Form 1023
17. Apply for federal recognition under Section 501(c)(3)
18. Complete required state charitable registrations
19. Obtain insurance
20. Establish safeguarding and volunteer policies
21. Establish homelessness and food-relief operating procedures
22. Establish educational programming
23. Establish correctional-institution partnership process
24. Establish property-acquisition controls
25. Begin property search only after acquisition requirements are established
26. Obtain all required approvals before operating any 24/7 location
