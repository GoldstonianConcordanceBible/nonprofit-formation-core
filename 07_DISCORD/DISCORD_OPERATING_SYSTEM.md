1. PURPOSE

Discord may be used by The Tabernacle for:

* Daily operations
* Team coordination
* Program communication
* Shift handoffs
* Property development
* Education coordination
* Prison/reentry coordination
* Food operations
* Volunteer coordination
* Board collaboration
* Announcements
* Project management

Discord is not the final legal record.

⸻

2. CORE CONTROL RULE

DISCORD = OPERATIONS

GITHUB = CONTROLLED DOCUMENTS

SECURE SYSTEM = CONFIDENTIAL RECORDS

A discussion in Discord does not automatically become:

* Board approval
* Corporate policy
* A signed contract
* A legal filing
* An authorized expenditure

When formal approval is required, the decision shall move into the appropriate corporate record.

20. INCIDENT RULE

Discord may be used to coordinate an active incident.

Discord should not become the permanent detailed incident-report system.

Workflow:

INCIDENT

↓

Protect people

↓

Emergency services if needed

↓

Leadership notification

↓

Secure incident report

↓

Review

↓

Corrective action

A short Discord notice may state:

Incident recorded. Secure report IR-2026-001 opened.

Do not paste the complete sensitive report into a general channel.

30. GITHUB INTEGRATION RULE

Discord discussion

↓

Draft document

↓

GitHub branch / pull request

↓

Review

↓

Required approval

↓

Merge to controlled branch

↓

Version/tag

↓

31. BOARD APPROVAL RULE

A Discord reaction such as:

👍

does not automatically constitute a legally valid Board vote.

Formal Board action must follow:

* Bylaws
* Applicable law
* Quorum
* Meeting or lawful written consent
* Minutes

⸻

32. PRIVATE MESSAGES

Organizational decisions should not routinely disappear into direct messages.

Where appropriate, important operational decisions should be summarized in the relevant organizational channel.

However, confidential matters shall use appropriate private channels or secure systems.

34. CHANNEL ARCHIVING

Inactive project channels should be:

1. Reviewed
2. Final decisions transferred to controlled records
3. Archived
4. Locked where appropriate

Do not delete channels containing relevant institutional history merely to reduce clutter without reviewing retention requirements.

⸻

35. NAMING STANDARD

Use lowercase channel names and hyphens.

Example:

#property-leads

not:

#Property Leads!!!

For controlled site identifiers:

#tab-001-shift-handoff

For property prospects:

#prop-001

Consistency supports scale.

36. NO CASE MANAGEMENT IN DISCORD

Discord shall not function as the primary case-management platform for:

* Homeless clients
* Prison participants
* Reentry participants
* Children
* Vulnerable adults
* Medical information

40. RELIGIOUS IDENTITY

The server belongs to an explicitly Christian organization.

Religious discussion, prayer, Scripture, ministry planning, and Christian teaching are expected within appropriate channels.

Operational disagreements shall remain professional.

⸻

41. EMERGENCY RULE

Discord is not guaranteed emergency infrastructure.

For:

* Fire
* Violence
* Serious medical emergency
* Immediate threat

personnel shall use emergency procedures first.

Do not wait for someone to answer Discord before calling emergency services when emergency contact is appropriate.

43. OFFBOARDING

When staff, contractors, or volunteers leave:

* Remove Discord roles
* Remove private-channel access
* Remove GitHub access where appropriate
* Remove email/system access
* Disable credentials
* Recover organizational devices/keys

Access should not remain because someone was once part of the organization.

⸻

44. PERIODIC ACCESS REVIEW

At least periodically, review:

* Administrators
* Board access
* Security access
* Safeguarding access
* Prison/reentry access
* Financial channels
* Property channels
* Former staff/volunteers

45. ADMINISTRATOR CONTROL

Keep full Discord administrator privileges to the minimum number of people operationally necessary.

Platform administration authority does not equal:

* Corporate authority
* Board authority
* Financial authority
* Policy authority

ADMIN ≠ GOVERNANCE
