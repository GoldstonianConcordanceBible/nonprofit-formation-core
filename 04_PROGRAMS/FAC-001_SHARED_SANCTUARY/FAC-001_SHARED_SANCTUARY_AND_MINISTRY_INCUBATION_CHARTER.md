⸻

1. PURPOSE

The Shared Sanctuary & Ministry Incubation Program exists to make church facilities available to Christian congregations, church plants, Bible-study groups, ministries, and related religious organizations that need appropriate space in which to:

* Worship
* Pray
* Teach Scripture
* Conduct Bible studies
* Develop congregations
* Train ministry leaders
* Hold fellowship activities
* Conduct religious education
* Carry out related ministry activities

The program is operated by:

THE TABERNACLE, INC.

It is not operated as a separately owned for-profit company.

⸻

2. CORE MISSION

The program seeks to provide an alternative to:

* Hotel conference rooms
* Temporary event venues
* School cafeterias
* Commercial meeting rooms
* Expensive single-congregation leases

by allowing multiple Christian ministries to responsibly share one religious facility.

⸻

3. PROGRAM MODEL

The Tabernacle acquires or leases a suitable property.

The Tabernacle retains:

* Ownership or master lease
* Facility control
* Scheduling authority

* Financial control

4. NONPROFIT STRUCTURE

The program remains entirely within:

THE TABERNACLE, INC.

Revenue is deposited into organizational accounts.

Revenue may support:

* Mortgage/rent
* Utilities
* Insurance
* Repairs
* Maintenance
* Ministry programs
* Food relief
* Education
* Reentry programs
* Staffing
* Property reserves
* Other lawful exempt activities

No individual possesses an ownership interest in FAC-001.

⸻

5. NO PROFIT DISTRIBUTION

Program surplus remains property of The Tabernacle, Inc.

It shall not be distributed to:

* Founder
* Directors
* Officers
* Participating pastors
* Congregations
* Employees

except through lawful:

* Compensation
* Reimbursements
* Contracts
* Charitable grants/assistance

properly authorized under organizational policy.

⸻

6. EXEMPT-PURPOSE DESIGN

FAC-001 is intended to advance religion by increasing access to physical infrastructure for:

* Worship
* Christian teaching
* Prayer
* Congregational development
* Religious education
* Ministry development

The program shall remain subordinate to and supportive of The Tabernacle’s exempt purposes.

⸻

7. COMMERCIAL ACTIVITY CONTROL

The Tabernacle shall monitor whether facility activity continues to further its exempt purposes.

The following are not automatically part of FAC-001:

* Commercial parties
* Corporate conferences
* Commercial entertainment
* For-profit event businesses
* General wedding/event venue operations
* Commercial catering
* Commercial studio rentals

Those activities require separate legal/tax review.
