2. PROGRAM PRINCIPLE

The Tabernacle is not simply selling vacant room hours.

FAC-001 exists to increase access to infrastructure for:

* Christian worship
* Bible study
* Prayer
* Christian education
* Ministry formation
* Congregational development
* Related religious activity

34. NO AUTOMATIC DENOMINATIONAL REQUIREMENT

FAC-001 may serve multiple Christian congregations and traditions.

51. NON-COMPENSATORY DECISION RULE

A serious failure in one critical category cannot be erased by strengths elsewhere.

Example:

Excellent mission fit does not overcome:

* Unsafe occupancy
* Missing required insurance
* Prohibited use
* Unmanageable safeguarding risk

STRONG MISSION FIT ≠ SAFETY WAIVER
