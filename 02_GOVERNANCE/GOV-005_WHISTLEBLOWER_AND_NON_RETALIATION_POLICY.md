ARTICLE I — PURPOSE

The Tabernacle, Inc. is committed to lawful, ethical, transparent, financially responsible, and safe operations.

This policy provides a protected method for reporting suspected:

* Fraud
* Theft
* Embezzlement
* Misuse of charitable assets
* Falsification of records
* Financial misconduct
* Abuse
* Neglect
* Exploitation
* Harassment
* Discrimination
* Retaliation
* Unsafe conditions
* Safeguarding violations
* Food-safety violations
* Security misconduct
* Prison or correctional-program violations
* Conflicts of interest
* Grant misuse
* Regulatory violations
* Violations of organizational policy
* Other unlawful or unethical conduct

⸻

ARTICLE II — PERSONS COVERED

This policy applies to:

* Directors
* Officers
* Founders
* Employees
* Ministers
* Chaplains
* Contractors
* Staffing-agency personnel
* Volunteers
* Interns
* Program leaders
* Committee members

The Board may extend reporting protections to other persons associated with the organization.

⸻

ARTICLE III — GOOD-FAITH REPORTS

A person who reasonably believes misconduct has occurred should report the matter in good faith.

A report does not need to prove wrongdoing.

The reporting person should provide truthful information and any facts reasonably available to them.

Good-faith mistakes shall not constitute misconduct merely because an allegation is ultimately unsubstantiated.

⸻

ARTICLE IV — KNOWINGLY FALSE REPORTS

This policy does not protect a person who knowingly makes a materially false accusation with malicious intent.

The distinction shall be made between:

GOOD-FAITH REPORT THAT PROVES INCORRECT

and

DELIBERATELY FALSE REPORT

They are not the same.

⸻

ARTICLE V — REPORTING CHANNELS

Concerns may ordinarily be reported to:

* Immediate supervisor
* Program Director
* Executive Director
* President/Chair
* Board Secretary
* Board-designated Compliance Officer
* Independent director designated by the Board

The organization should eventually establish a dedicated reporting address such as:

compliance@<domain>

or

integrity@<domain>

⸻

ARTICLE VI — REPORTS INVOLVING MANAGEMENT

If a concern involves the Executive Director or senior executive leadership, the report should bypass that person and go directly to:

* Board Chair
* Independent director
* Governance Committee
* Outside counsel where appropriate

⸻

ARTICLE VII — REPORTS INVOLVING THE BOARD CHAIR

If a concern involves the Board Chair, the report should be made to:

* Vice Chair
* Board Secretary
* Another independent director
* Governance Committee
* Outside counsel where appropriate

No officer shall control the investigation of allegations involving themselves.

⸻

ARTICLE VIII — REPORTS INVOLVING FOUNDERS

Founder status does not alter the reporting process.

Allegations involving a founder shall be reviewed through the same independent governance process applicable to other individuals.

FOUNDER ≠ IMMUNITY

⸻

ARTICLE IX — EMERGENCIES

This policy does not replace emergency reporting.

Immediate danger to human life or safety may require contacting:

* 911
* Police
* Fire department
* Emergency medical services
* Child protective services
* Adult protective services
* Correctional authorities
* Other appropriate authorities

Internal reporting should not delay emergency action.

⸻

ARTICLE X — MANDATORY REPORTING

Nothing in this policy shall prohibit or delay legally required reporting of suspected:

* Child abuse
* Abuse of vulnerable adults
* Sexual abuse
* Certain threats
* Other conduct subject to mandatory reporting requirements

The Tabernacle shall comply with applicable mandatory-reporting laws.
