ARTICLE I — PURPOSE

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that compensation, reimbursements, contracts, leases, purchases, and other economic transactions involving The Tabernacle, Inc. are:

* Reasonable
* Necessary
* Properly authorized
* Properly documented
* Consistent with charitable purposes
* Free from improper private benefit
* Conducted at fair and supportable terms

The existence of a family, founder, employment, or business relationship does not automatically prohibit a transaction.

It does require appropriate disclosure and independent review.

⸻

ARTICLE II — CORE RULE

WORK MAY BE PAID

A founder, officer, employee, director, family member, contractor, or other person may receive compensation for legitimate services actually performed when legally permissible.

STATUS ALONE SHALL NOT BE PAID

Compensation shall not be paid merely because a person is:

* A founder
* A director
* A donor
* A relative of a founder
* A minister
* A friend
* A supporter

Compensation must correspond to actual services or another legitimate transaction.

⸻

ARTICLE III — REASONABLE COMPENSATION

Compensation should not exceed the amount ordinarily paid for comparable services under comparable circumstances.

Factors may include:

* Position
* Responsibilities
* Hours worked
* Experience
* Education
* Specialized skills
* Geographic market
* Organizational size
* Budget
* Number of employees supervised
* Complexity of operations
* Comparable nonprofit compensation
* Comparable religious organization compensation
* Comparable private-sector compensation where relevant

All economic benefits should be considered when evaluating total compensation.

⸻

ARTICLE IV — FORMS OF COMPENSATION

Compensation may include:

* Salary
* Hourly wages
* Contract fees
* Bonuses
* Stipends
* Housing benefits
* Vehicle benefits
* Health insurance
* Retirement benefits
* Deferred compensation
* Severance
* Educational benefits
* Paid travel
* Personal use of organizational property
* Other economic benefits

Compensation shall not be disguised through reimbursement, property use, or other arrangements.

⸻

ARTICLE V — FOUNDER COMPENSATION

Founders may be compensated for actual work performed for The Tabernacle.

Possible compensated founder roles may include:

* Executive Director
* President
* Minister
* Program Director
* Education Director
* Property Director
* Operations Director
* Other bona fide positions

Founder compensation requires independent Board review.

The compensated founder shall not vote on their own compensation.

⸻

ARTICLE VI — ELISE AND OTHER FAMILY EMPLOYMENT

Family members may work for The Tabernacle when there is a legitimate organizational need.

Examples may include:

* Food program leadership
* Recipe development
* Kitchen management
* Education
* Administration
* Ministry
* Outreach
* Other legitimate work

Employment or contracting must be based on actual duties rather than family relationship.

The Board or authorized disinterested decision-makers shall establish compensation using reasonable market information.

⸻

ARTICLE VII — JOB DESCRIPTIONS

Every compensated executive or key employee should have a written job description.

The description should include:

* Job title
* Supervisor
* Responsibilities
* Authority
* Expected schedule
* Employment classification
* Compensation
* Performance expectations

Job descriptions should be reviewed periodically.

⸻

ARTICLE VIII — APPROVAL OF INSIDER COMPENSATION

Compensation involving a founder, officer, key employee, director, or other person capable of exercising substantial influence shall be approved using the following procedure:

1. Proposed compensation is documented.
2. The interested person discloses the relationship.
3. The interested person does not vote.
4. Disinterested decision-makers review appropriate compensation data.
5. The decision-makers determine whether the arrangement is reasonable.
6. The arrangement is approved before payment begins whenever practicable.
7. The decision is documented contemporaneously.

⸻

ARTICLE IX — COMPARABILITY DATA

Comparability information may include:

* Compensation surveys
* Salary databases
* Comparable nonprofit organizations
* Comparable churches
* Similar charitable organizations
* Similar jobs in the geographic area
* Independent compensation studies
* Written employment offers
* Qualified consultant analysis

The Board should retain the data supporting its determination.

⸻

ARTICLE X — DOCUMENTATION

Minutes approving compensation should record:

* Person receiving compensation
* Position
* Duties
* Amount
* Benefits
* Effective date
* Comparable organizations or data reviewed
* Directors present
* Directors voting
* Conflicts disclosed
* Recusals
* Reason for approval

⸻

ARTICLE XI — DIRECTOR COMPENSATION

Directors should generally serve in their governance capacity without compensation unless the Board determines otherwise and applicable law permits compensation.

A director may separately receive reasonable compensation for actual professional or employment services provided outside normal Board duties.

Such arrangements require conflict review.

⸻

ARTICLE XII — REIMBURSEMENT OF EXPENSES

The Tabernacle may reimburse legitimate business expenses including:

* Travel
* Mileage
* Lodging
* Meals during authorized travel
* Supplies
* Program purchases
* Training
* Professional expenses

Reimbursement requires appropriate documentation.

Personal expenses shall not be submitted as organizational expenses.

⸻

ARTICLE XIII — ACCOUNTABLE REIMBURSEMENT PRINCIPLE

Expense reimbursement should require:

1. Legitimate organizational purpose
2. Timely expense submission
3. Receipt or other appropriate evidence
4. Identification of the program or business purpose
5. Return of unused advances when applicable

⸻

ARTICLE XIV — CREDIT AND DEBIT CARDS

Organizational credit or debit cards may be issued only to authorized individuals.

Cards shall not be used for:

* Personal purchases
* Personal loans
* Personal cash advances
* Unapproved gifts
* Expenses unrelated to The Tabernacle

Receipts shall be retained.

The cardholder should not be the sole person reviewing their own transactions.

⸻

ARTICLE XV — RELATED-PARTY BUSINESSES

The Tabernacle may contract with a business owned by:

* A founder
* Director
* Officer
* Employee
* Family member

only when the arrangement:

* Has been fully disclosed
* Is lawful
* Serves a legitimate organizational purpose
* Is reasonably priced
* Is independently approved
* Is documented

⸻

ARTICLE XVI — COMPETING QUOTES

For material related-party purchases, The Tabernacle should seek competing quotes or other market evidence where reasonably practicable.

The Board may document why competitive bidding was impractical when applicable.

Lowest price alone is not required.

The Board may consider:

* Quality
* Experience
* Reliability
* Availability
* Mission needs
* Total cost
* Performance

⸻

ARTICLE XVII — PROPERTY PURCHASES

When purchasing property from a related party, The Tabernacle should ordinarily obtain appropriate evidence of fair market value.

This may include:

* Independent appraisal
* Broker opinion
* Comparable sales
* Other qualified valuation

The interested person shall not control the approval.

⸻

ARTICLE XVIII — PROPERTY LEASES

A property lease involving a related party must specify:

* Premises
* Rent
* Term
* Utilities
* Maintenance
* Repairs
* Insurance
* Taxes
* Improvements
* Termination provisions

The Board must determine that the arrangement is reasonable and serves The Tabernacle.

⸻

ARTICLE XIX — DONATED PROPERTY

A founder or related person may donate property.

The organization shall not promise a donor a particular tax deduction.

The donor is responsible for obtaining their own tax advice and valuation when required.

Acceptance remains subject to organizational due diligence.

⸻

ARTICLE XX — LOANS TO THE ORGANIZATION

A founder, director, or other person may lend money to The Tabernacle only where lawful and approved by disinterested directors.

The arrangement should document:

* Principal
* Interest
* Term
* Payment schedule
* Security
* Default provisions
* Other material terms

Terms must be fair to the organization.

⸻

ARTICLE XXI — LOANS FROM THE ORGANIZATION

Loans from The Tabernacle to directors, officers, founders, employees, or related persons shall not be made unless:

* Applicable law permits the loan
* Counsel confirms legality where appropriate
* The transaction furthers legitimate organizational interests
* Independent Board approval occurs

The organization should generally avoid insider loans.

⸻

ARTICLE XXII — STAFFING COMPANIES

The Tabernacle may use staffing companies for functions such as:

* Kitchen operations
* Facilities support
* Cleaning
* Administrative work
* Events
* Other temporary labor

Security staffing shall additionally satisfy applicable licensing, training, insurance, and regulatory requirements.

Staffing arrangements shall be governed by written agreements where appropriate.

⸻

ARTICLE XXIII — EMPLOYEES VS CONTRACTORS

Worker classification shall depend on applicable law and the actual working relationship.

The organization shall not label a worker an independent contractor merely to avoid:

* Payroll taxes
* Overtime
* Workers’ compensation
* Benefits obligations
* Employment protections

Professional review may be obtained when classification is uncertain.

⸻

ARTICLE XXIV — SECURITY PERSONNEL

Security personnel may be:

* Employees
* Personnel supplied through licensed staffing/security firms
* Other lawful workers

The organization shall verify applicable:

* Licensing
* Registration
* Training
* Insurance
* Background requirements

No worker may perform duties legally requiring a license without satisfying those requirements.

⸻

ARTICLE XXV — FOOD PROGRAM PERSONNEL

Food-service personnel may include:

* Employees
* Contractors
* Staffing-agency workers
* Volunteers

All persons shall follow:

* Approved recipes
* Food-safety procedures
* Allergen procedures
* Sanitation requirements
* Applicable health regulations
* Supervisor instructions

⸻

ARTICLE XXVI — MINISTERS AND CHAPLAINS

Ministers, chaplains, and religious-program personnel may receive reasonable compensation for actual services.

Compensation shall not be based merely on spiritual title or founder status.

Special tax rules applicable to clergy should be reviewed with a qualified tax professional.

⸻

ARTICLE XXVII — PRISON AND REENTRY PERSONNEL

Personnel working in correctional programs may be employees, contractors, ministers, educators, mentors, or volunteers.

Compensation does not create authority to enter or operate in a correctional institution.

Facility approval remains independently required.

⸻

ARTICLE XXVIII — EDUCATIONAL PERSONNEL

The Tabernacle may compensate:

* Lecturers
* Teachers
* Course designers
* Tutors
* Mentors
* Curriculum developers
* Technology instructors
* Other educators

The organization shall determine whether it owns, licenses, or merely has permission to use instructional materials created by compensated personnel.

⸻

ARTICLE XXIX — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Contracts involving paid creation of:

* Courses
* Lectures
* Videos
* Curriculum
* Books
* Software
* Training materials
* Designs
* Media

should state who owns the resulting intellectual property and what rights The Tabernacle receives.

⸻

ARTICLE XXX — CHARITABLE PROPERTY

Property purchased with charitable funds belongs to The Tabernacle.

This includes, as applicable:

* Buildings
* Furniture
* Kitchen equipment
* Vehicles
* Computers
* Cameras
* Security equipment
* Educational equipment
* Supplies

Organizational assets shall not be converted to personal assets without lawful authorization and appropriate consideration.

⸻

ARTICLE XXXI — PERSONAL USE OF ASSETS

Limited incidental personal use of organizational property may be allowed under Board-approved policy.

Material personal use may constitute compensation and must be appropriately documented.

⸻

ARTICLE XXXII — ADVANCES

Cash advances should be minimized.

Where an advance is necessary:

* Purpose must be documented
* Recipient must account for expenditures
* Receipts must be supplied
* Excess funds must be returned promptly

⸻

ARTICLE XXXIII — CASH PAYMENTS

Cash payments should be avoided when practical.

When cash is necessary, documentation should identify:

* Date
* Amount
* Recipient
* Purpose
* Program
* Approver

⸻

ARTICLE XXXIV — SEGREGATION OF DUTIES

Whenever practical, different people should perform:

AUTHORIZE

PAY

RECORD

RECONCILE

No single person should exercise unchecked control over the complete financial cycle.

⸻

ARTICLE XXXV — LARGE TRANSACTIONS

The Board shall establish approval thresholds.

Example framework:

Routine Operating Expense

Executive approval within budget.

Significant Expense

Dual approval.

Major Contract

Executive plus Board or designated committee approval.

Real Estate

Board approval required.

Related-Party Transaction

Independent review required regardless of ordinary approval threshold when material.

Exact dollar limits shall be established after The Tabernacle adopts its initial budget.

⸻

ARTICLE XXXVI — EXCESS BENEFIT TRANSACTIONS

The organization shall avoid transactions in which an insider receives economic value greater than the value The Tabernacle receives.

When evaluating transactions involving persons with substantial influence, The Tabernacle shall seek to establish appropriate evidence that compensation and other economic transactions are reasonable and at fair market value.

⸻

ARTICLE XXXVII — CORRECTIVE ACTION

If an excessive or improper payment is discovered, the Board shall evaluate appropriate corrective action.

Actions may include:

* Repayment
* Return of property
* Contract adjustment
* Termination
* Tax reporting
* Professional consultation
* Other corrective action

⸻

ARTICLE XXXVIII — NO AUTOMATIC ENTITLEMENT

No person has an automatic right to:

* Salary
* Housing
* Vehicle
* Reimbursement
* Contract
* Vendor work
* Property transaction
* Loan
* Grant
* Other economic benefit

because they founded, supported, volunteered for, donated to, or served The Tabernacle.

⸻

ARTICLE XXXIX — ANNUAL REVIEW

At least annually, the Board should review:

* Executive compensation
* Founder compensation
* Related-party contracts
* Family employment
* Property arrangements
* Major vendor contracts
* Significant benefits
* Reimbursement practices

⸻

ARTICLE XL — CONTROL PRINCIPLE

SERVICE MAY BE COMPENSATED

STATUS IS NOT COMPENSATION

RELATED PARTY ≠ AUTOMATICALLY PROHIBITED

RELATED PARTY = DISCLOSE + REVIEW + DOCUMENT

NONPROFIT MONEY ≠ PERSONAL MONEY

NONPROFIT PROPERTY ≠ PERSONAL PROPERTY

AUTHORIZE ≠ PAY ≠ RECORD ≠ RECONCILE
